# Sales Documents

Quotations and billing documents track a client job from proposal through payment.

## Language

**Won**:
A quotation the client has accepted, whether or not work has started.
_Avoid_: Paid

**Ongoing**:
An accepted client job with work underway. On a quotation or pro forma, this does not mean payment has been received.
_Avoid_: Partially paid, unpaid

**Paid**:
A billing document whose requested payment has been received.
_Avoid_: Ongoing

# Finance Operations

Finance operations connect sales, purchases, expenses, and their actual payments without duplicating balances.

## Language

**Project**:
A client-owned job used to group sales documents, purchase orders, expenses, and payments.
_Avoid_: Reference, Cost centre

**Receivable**:
The unpaid portion of an issued tax invoice after issued receipts allocated to it.
_Avoid_: Invoice total, Sales

**Payable**:
The unpaid portion of an active purchase order or posted expense after recorded payments.
_Avoid_: Expense total, Purchase total

**Receipt**:
Evidence that money was received from a client. Only an issued receipt counts as received cash.
_Avoid_: Paid status, Invoice

**Expense**:
A non-purchase-order cost such as salary, petrol, rent, utilities, or an incidental client cost.
_Avoid_: Purchase order, Payment

**Net cash**:
Issued receipt amounts minus purchase and expense payments in a period.
_Avoid_: Profit, Project margin

**Project margin**:
Invoiced sales excluding VAT minus project purchase and expense costs excluding recoverable VAT.
_Avoid_: Net cash, Bank balance
